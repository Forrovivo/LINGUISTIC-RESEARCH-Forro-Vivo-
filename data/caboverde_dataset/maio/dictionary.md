# Kabuverdianu of Maio dictionary

**Language:** Kabuverdianu of Maio  
**Island:** Maio  
**Group:** Sotavento  
**ISO 639-3:** kea  
**Translation pairs:** Kabuverdianu of Maio → Portuguese; Kabuverdianu of Maio → English
**Project started:** 23 March 2023  

This file is a **Maio-only** Cabo Verdean Creole dataset. It is not a website and not an application.

Do not copy headwords from other Cabo Verdean island folders (santiago, fogo, brava, saovicente, santoantao, saonicolau, sal, boavista).
Do not copy Guinea-Bissau Kriol, Forro, Angolar, or Lung’Ie.

Entries were isolated from Maio-labelled forms in Ana Karina Tavares Moreira 2014 (in Lang ed.). Santiago comparison forms were not copied. Portuguese word glosses follow that chapter. English is null when the source does not print it.

Further Maio-labelled lemmas were isolated from Moreira 2014 morphology and derivation tables. Santiago comparison forms were not copied.

No lexicon has been copied from other folders. Add a headword only when a cited source labels it as the creole of Maio.
