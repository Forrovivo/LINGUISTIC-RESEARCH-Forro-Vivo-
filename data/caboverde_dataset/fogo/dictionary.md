# Kabuverdianu of Fogo dictionary

**Language:** Kabuverdianu of Fogo  
**Island:** Fogo  
**Group:** Sotavento  
**ISO 639-3:** kea  
**Translation pairs:** Kabuverdianu of Fogo → Portuguese; Kabuverdianu of Fogo → English
**Project started:** 23 March 2023  

This file is a **Fogo-only** Cabo Verdean Creole dataset. It is not a website and not an application.

Do not copy headwords from other Cabo Verdean island folders (santiago, maio, brava, saovicente, santoantao, saonicolau, sal, boavista).
Do not copy Guinea-Bissau Kriol, Forro, Angolar, or Lung’Ie.

Entries were isolated from Fogo-labelled cells in Raimundo Tavares Lopes 2014 (in Lang ed.) and from Fogo-labelled examples in Souza 2007. Santiago, Santo Antão, and São Vicente columns were not copied. Portuguese word glosses follow those sources. English is null when the source does not print it.

Further Fogo-labelled lemmas were isolated from Ana Karina Tavares Moreira 2020 (INALCO). Santiago comparison forms and continental creole cells were not copied.

No lexicon has been copied from other folders. Add a headword only when a cited source labels it as the creole of Fogo.
