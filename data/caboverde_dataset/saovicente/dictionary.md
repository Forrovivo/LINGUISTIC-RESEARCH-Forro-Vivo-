# Kabuverdianu of São Vicente dictionary

**Language:** Kabuverdianu of São Vicente  
**Island:** São Vicente  
**Group:** Barlavento  
**ISO 639-3:** kea  
**Translation pairs:** Kabuverdianu of São Vicente → Portuguese; Kabuverdianu of São Vicente → English
**Project started:** 23 March 2023  

This file is a **São Vicente-only** Cabo Verdean Creole dataset. It is not a website and not an application.

Do not copy headwords from other Cabo Verdean island folders (santiago, fogo, maio, brava, santoantao, saonicolau, sal, boavista).
Do not copy Guinea-Bissau Kriol, Forro, Angolar, or Lung’Ie.

Entries were isolated from APiCS Online language 32 (Dominika Swolkien 2013), which is labelled as the creole of São Vicente. 
Survey italic+gloss pairs and example interlinear leftovers were used. Grammatical labels were not stored as dictionary senses. 
Portuguese word glosses are null in this pass. Portuguese free translations of examples are stored when APiCS gives them. Other island folders were not used as evidence.

Further São Vicente-labelled lemmas were isolated from Swolkien 2015 and from leftover APiCS 32 survey loans. Santiago comparison forms were not copied.
