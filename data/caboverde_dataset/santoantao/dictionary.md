# Kabuverdianu of Santo Antão dictionary

**Language:** Kabuverdianu of Santo Antão  
**Island:** Santo Antão  
**Group:** Barlavento  
**ISO 639-3:** kea  
**Translation pairs:** Kabuverdianu of Santo Antão → Portuguese; Kabuverdianu of Santo Antão → English
**Project started:** 23 March 2023  

This file is a **Santo Antão-only** Cabo Verdean Creole dataset. It is not a website and not an application.

Do not copy headwords from other Cabo Verdean island folders (santiago, fogo, maio, brava, saovicente, saonicolau, sal, boavista).
Do not copy Guinea-Bissau Kriol, Forro, Angolar, or Lung’Ie.

Entries were isolated from Santo Antão-labelled forms in Swolkien & Cobbinah 2019, Maria do Céu dos Santos Baptista 2014 (in Lang ed.), and Souza 2007. São Vicente, São Nicolau, Santiago, and Fogo columns were not copied. English or Portuguese is stored only when that source prints it.

No lexicon has been copied from other folders. Add a headword only when a cited source labels it as the creole of Santo Antão.
