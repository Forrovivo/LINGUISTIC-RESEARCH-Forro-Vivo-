# Angola Contruy sources

This folder documents **Angola Contruy** (Angola the country). It is **not** Angolar / Ngola.

Angolar / Ngola is a São Tomé creole. Its sources and entries stay in `data/saotome_dataset/angolar/sources.md` and `data/saotome_dataset/angolar/dictionary.json`. Do not copy them here.

Angola Contruy collection starts on **23 April 2025** (**2 years 1 month** after the project start of **23 March 2023**). The lexicon stays empty until a cited source names Angola Contruy.

APiCS Online language 36 (Maurer 2013, Angolar) is not Angola Contruy evidence.

Kimbundu, Umbundu, and Angolan Portuguese are not Angola Contruy headwords unless a source labels them as such. They are not Angolar headwords either.

## Missing-term protocol

Use this folder’s `TERM_NOT_FOUND` object. Do not fall back to Angolar, Forro, Lung’Ie, Cabo Verdean, or Guinea-Bissau Kriol.
