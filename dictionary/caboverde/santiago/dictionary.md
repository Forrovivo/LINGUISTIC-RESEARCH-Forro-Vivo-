# Kabuverdianu of Santiago dictionary

**Language:** Kabuverdianu of Santiago  
**Island:** Santiago  
**Group:** Sotavento  
**ISO 639-3:** kea  
**Translation pairs:** Kabuverdianu of Santiago → Portuguese; Kabuverdianu of Santiago → English
**Project started:** 23 March 2023  

This file is a **Santiago-only** Cabo Verdean Creole dataset. It is not a website and not an application.

Do not copy headwords from other Cabo Verdean island folders (fogo, maio, brava, saovicente, santoantao, saonicolau, sal, boavista).
Do not copy Guinea-Bissau Kriol, Forro, Angolar, or Lung’Ie.

Entries were isolated from APiCS Online language 30 (Jürgen Lang 2013), which is labelled as the creole of Santiago. 
Survey italic+gloss pairs and example interlinear leftovers were used. Grammatical labels were not stored as dictionary senses. 
Portuguese word glosses are null in this pass. Portuguese free translations of examples are stored when APiCS gives them. Other island folders were not used as evidence.

If a requested Santiago term is not in this dataset:

```json
{"status": "error", "code": "TERM_NOT_FOUND", "message": "Translation not available in the verified Kabuverdianu of Santiago database."}
```

Machine-readable copy: `dictionary.json`. Source record: `sources.md`.
Parent index: `../dictionary.md`.
